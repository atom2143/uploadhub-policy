UploadHub Privacy Policy

UploadHub is a personal creator workflow tool used to schedule and publish creator-owned video content.

UploadHub stores uploaded media, captions, hashtags, scheduling metadata, and platform authentication tokens only for the purpose of content scheduling and publishing.

UploadHub does not sell, share, or distribute user data to third parties except when authorized by the user for publishing content through connected platform APIs.

Uploaded content and tokens are stored on private, self-hosted infrastructure controlled by the creator.

Access may be revoked at any time through connected platform account settings.
